OpenFESL is an unofficial, community-run replacement for the discontinued online services of this game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts.

The service stores the data its online features need: the account name, its public display name, a hash of the password, the country, the accepted version of these terms and what the game saves through the service, such as profiles and statistics. An account that links a sign-in service also keeps that service's id for the account, its name there and when it was linked. The public IP address of the computer that hosts a game is shown in game lists, and the public IP address of a computer that joins a game is passed to the computer that hosts it. Where the operator sets it up, Cloudflare Turnstile checks the website's forms that take an e-mail address or send mail, to tell people from automated requests; Cloudflare then receives the visitor's IP address and browser details.

The service is provided as is, without warranty, and may change or end at any time. Accounts used for cheating or abuse may be suspended. Account deletion can be requested on the account page or from the operator of this service.
